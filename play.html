BLOCKCRAFT

Survive, craft better tools, and dig deep enough to find diamonds.

A / D or ← → to move · Shift to sprint · W, Space or ↑ to jump
Hold left click to mine or hit · Right click to place
1–9 or mouse wheel for the hotbar · C or E to open crafting · M to mute
Right click with an apple in hand to eat it · Jump + right-click below you to pillar up
Stone needs a pickaxe. Zombies come out at night, so build a shelter and light it with torches.
Your world saves automatically. Press R for a new world. Press ? or Esc to close this help.
